Hemini Learn — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 17, 2026

Hemini Learn is an educational application designed to help students study and revise CBSE curriculum content for Classes 9–12.

1. Information We Collect

Hemini Learn does not require account creation and does not collect personal identifiers such as email address, phone number, contacts, or precise location.

The app does not request access to the camera, microphone, contacts, storage/media, Bluetooth, SMS, or phone-state information.

2. AI Study Assistant

When you use the AI Study Assistant, the text you enter is sent to Google's Gemini service to generate an educational response. This may include your study question, selected subject, class level, preferred language, and requested explanation depth.

Hemini Learn does not send audio or images to the AI service.

3. Local Storage

Chat history, saved notes and bookmarks, quiz history and progress, and certain app preferences may be stored locally on your device.

This information is not uploaded by Hemini Learn to its own server.

4. Deleting Local Data

You can remove locally stored app data by clearing the app's storage/data from your device settings or by uninstalling the application.

5. Firebase App Check

Hemini Learn uses Firebase App Check to help verify legitimate app requests and reduce abuse. App Check may use an attestation token associated with the app/device environment.

6. Advertising and Analytics

Hemini Learn does not display advertisements and does not use advertising or analytics SDKs for tracking users.

7. Payments

Hemini Learn does not require payment information and does not provide paid subscriptions or in-app purchases.

8. Data Security

Network communication used by the application is transmitted over HTTPS. Local study information is stored on the user's device.

9. Student Privacy

Hemini Learn is an educational application intended for students. The app does not require account creation and does not knowingly request personal information from students.

10. Third-Party Services

The application uses Google's Gemini service for AI-generated educational responses and Firebase App Check for application integrity and abuse prevention.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
